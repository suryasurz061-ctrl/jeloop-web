Jeloop was inspired by the idea of creating a unique and enjoyable dessert, as many people tend to get bored with ordinary desserts. The name Jeloop comes from “Jelly Loop,” representing the hope that customers will keep coming back for more and that Jeloop will become a comfort dessert they always crave and remember.

About Us

Jeloop is an F&B business that introduces an innovative tea-based dessert bowl, combining tea, jelly, butterfly pea flower boba, and creamy milk tea. It offers two variants: Signature TeaQ, with a rich roasted oolong aroma, and Tropical TeaQ, featuring a refreshing blend of strawberries and kombucha jelly. Guided by the slogan “Crafted From Tea, Made for Comfort,” Jeloop offers a unique and enjoyable dessert experience.
